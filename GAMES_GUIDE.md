# 🎮 Interactive Games Guide

## Memory Card Game 🧠
**What it does:** Classic matching game where you flip cards to find pairs

**How to play:**
- Click cards to flip them
- Remember card positions
- Match all pairs
- Lower moves = better score

**Difficulty Levels:**
- 🟦 Easy: 4 pairs (8 cards)
- 🟧 Hard: 6 pairs (12 cards)

**Scoring:**
- Fewer moves = "Perfect Memory"
- 10-16 moves = "Good job"
- More moves = "Getting there!"

---

## Number Guessing Game 🎯
**What it does:** I think of a number, you guess it

**How to play:**
- I think of number 1-100
- You make guesses
- I tell you if you're hot or cold
- Guess faster for more respect

**Temperature System:**
- 🔥 BURNING HOT (0-2 away)
- 🌡️ Very Hot (3-5 away)
- 🔥 Hot (6-10 away)
- 🌤️ Warm (11-20 away)
- ☀️ Lukewarm (21-35 away)
- ❄️ Cold (36-50 away)
- 🧊 Freezing (51+ away)

**Scoring:**
- ≤5 attempts: Genius Level
- 6-8 attempts: Great job
- 9-12 attempts: Not bad
- 12+ attempts: Persistence is key!

---

## Math Challenge 📐
**What it does:** Solve 10 math problems in 30 seconds each

**How to play:**
- Get 10 problems in a row
- Each problem = 30 seconds
- Faster answers = more points
- Build streaks to unlock harder levels

**Difficulty Progression:**
- 🟩 Easy (5 attempts): Addition & Subtraction (1-12)
- 🟨 Medium (5+ streak): Add Multiplication (1-25)
- 🟥 Hard (10+ streak): Add Division (1-50)

**Scoring System:**
- Base points: 10
- Minus points: (30 - seconds_left) / 10
- Max points per problem: ~10
- Max total: ~100

**Final Ratings:**
- 120+ points: 🧠 Genius Mathematician
- 90-119 points: 👍 Impressive
- 60-89 points: 😌 Not bad
- <60 points: 😅 Math is hard

## Game Tips & Tricks 💡

### Memory Game:
- Look for patterns in card placement
- Try to memorize in groups
- Concentrate on the hard mode for challenge
- Lower attempts = higher respect

### Number Guessing:
- Start with 50 (divide range in half)
- Use hot/cold feedback strategically
- Less attempts = more points
- Hint appears after 10 attempts

### Math Challenge:
- Speed matters more than accuracy
- Build streaks for difficulty unlock
- Harder problems = easier streak building
- Practice during first few problems
